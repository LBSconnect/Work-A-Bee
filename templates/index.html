Frequently Asked Questions

Is Work-A-Beez free to use?

Work-A-Beez is free for your first 14 days on any plan. A card is required to get started, but you won't be charged until your 14 days are up, and you can cancel anytime. See our Pricing page for details.

How does employee clock-in work?

Employees clock in and out from a simple PIN-based screen on office computers your admin authorizes as trusted devices, which helps prevent buddy punching and time theft.

Can Work-A-Beez handle payroll for hourly and contract workers?

Yes. Work-A-Beez tracks hours for both employees and contractors, calculates pay based on your rates and overtime rules, and generates weekly payroll reports you can email directly to your team.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Work-A-Beez runs entirely in your browser, with no downloads, no hardware, and no setup beyond registering the computers you want employees to clock in from.

Is my company's data secure?

Yes. Only devices your admin explicitly registers can be used to clock in or out, and each company's data is kept isolated from other businesses using Work-A-Beez.
